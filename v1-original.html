How I Operate as a Designer

  • Managing designers means my job is to make the team better, not just the work. I invest in clear processes, honest feedback, and giving ownership early because a designer who's growing is a designer who stays.
  • I don't wait for clarity, I create it. Ambiguity is part of the job, and I'd rather ask uncomfortable questions early than ship something wrong.
  • Design, for me, is about removing ambiguity. If something needs a long explanation, it's already broken.
  • I take a clear position and expect my team to do the same. Strong opinions matter, but the moment better reasoning shows up, we move on without ego.
  • I think in systems, not screens. Flows, rules, and edge cases matter more to me than one-off visuals that won't survive scale.
  • I design with business reality in mind. Good UX should reduce risk, save time, and move real metrics, not just look good in a deck.
